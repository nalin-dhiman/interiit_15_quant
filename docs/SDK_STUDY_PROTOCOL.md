Protocol first written 2026-10-15 before reading any held-out price values. The held-out file was inspected only for its SHA-256, bytes, header and row count. This protocol concerns the supplied **development demo**, not an undisclosed competition round. ## Primary estimand and scope

The primary comparison is R2 minus the strongest of tuned B1/B2/B3/B3_factor selected using training-file validation blocks. R3 and direct response adaptation are unavailable: price CSVs do not identify a live competitor/customer response model, and this study must not relabel known demo mechanics as an estimated competition kernel. B0 is the unmodified provided participant. All official Engine accounting, matching, penalties, customer generation, prices, settlement and RNG implementations are used without source changes. Dealer callbacks are executed with synchronous acknowledgements and zero network latency, according to the organiser Runner's event ordering; this is an offline execution scenario, not a replacement exchange or a latency-certified competition simulation. Network runner/pre-flight validation is separate.

A session is 12 intervals, 9 price ticks followed by one official `advance()` per interval, then 3 final-window ticks and official `finish()`: 123 transitions and 124 rows. Its closing duration is the published 2 seconds at 0.5 seconds per update; the deadline takes precedence over the potential fourth final tick. Price blocks start at 0,124,...,992, giving 9 nonoverlapping 124-row blocks in each 1,201-row file. No frozen-price end-of-file sessions are allowed.

## Tuning and sealing

Training uses only `demo_data.csv`. Blocks 0--5, seeds 11 and 29 tune each of B1, B2, B3, B3_factor and R2 with 12 candidate configurations each, 144 sessions per method. The identical quote grid is edge [0.04,0.08,0.12,0.16] times skew [0,0.002,0.005]; B3/R2 use the same prospectively recorded 12 joint quote/hedge configurations rather than a full Cartesian hedge grid. Their band fractions cycle through [0.2,0.4,0.6], targets through [0,0.5]. This is an equal evaluation budget and a limited search, not a globally optimal conventional baseline. B0 is an untuned reference. All policies use zero empirical price drift and explicitly supplied demo settlement B=2*A. No hidden limits or opponent quotes enter features.

Select each method's candidate by mean raw ledger PnL on tuning sessions, ties by its predeclared candidate index. Training validation blocks 6--8, seeds 101,211,307,401 select the strongest conventional fallback from B1/B2/B3/B3_factor (ties B1,B2,B3,B3_factor order). Validation also provides pilot paired R2-versus-fallback block variance. After pilot results are written, a seal records exact candidate parameters, training/source hashes and final inference choices **before holdout price values are opened**. R2 with B3's quote parameters is a separate fixed-quote hedge ablation and receives no extra tuning. It uses the selected R2 continuation parameters but overrides quote edge/skew with B3's selected values.

## Materiality, sample size and inference

Materiality is fixed at +1.0 price-currency unit mean raw PnL per 12-interval session. Approximate required independent blocks for 80% power at one-sided alpha 0.05 are computed from training validation block-difference standard deviation as ceil(((1.645+0.842)*sd/1.0)^2). This is a pilot normal approximation with only 3 blocks, not a reliable power guarantee. At most 9 held-out independent price blocks are available. Repeated seeds on a price block are paired repetitions, **not independent new price paths**. Report insufficient power when the pilot required block count exceeds 9; no seed increase can remove that limitation.

The final uses held-out blocks 0--8 and seeds 1009,2017,3011,4001, after policy sealing. Primary inference is a paired block sign-randomisation test across the 9 block-mean differences (all 512 sign assignments), and a descriptive paired block bootstrap 95% interval with fixed bootstrap seed 7301. Exchangeability/symmetry for the sign test and independent representative price blocks for inference are assumptions; sequential blocks from one simulated path may remain dependent. These outputs therefore establish only a narrowly scoped demo diagnostic. The primary superiority claim requires positive mean, sign-test p<=0.05, lower bootstrap endpoint>0 and materiality >=1; otherwise report inconclusive/not supported as appropriate. The fixed-quote and other policy comparisons are exploratory and receive no confirmatory claims. Tail summaries and worst block are descriptive; 9 blocks cannot power a tail-risk claim.

## Calibration and failures

Record exactly one final observation for every RFQ/session/policy, including final acknowledged own quote, public final outcome, public winner/price and matching-time reference. Losing outcomes do not reveal hidden customer limits; model the joint event **winning and eligibility** through observed trade status. B1 observations from all tuning candidates fit pooled and regularised logistic fill models; validate on the selected B1 on training-file validation blocks. Conditional one-update post-fill markouts are measured using subsequent observed price, never current features. Expose support and calibration errors; do not deploy unsupported transfer. No holdout refitting, seed exclusion, policy replacement or objective changes are allowed. Preserve crashes, rejections, penalties and all tuning trials. Scoring ranks/volume points are outside this raw-PnL experiment.

Separate random generators are the organiser Engine's price/customer/tie generators and independently seeded example-opponent generators. Original tie RNG draws are conditional on eligibility, so counterfactual policies can change tie draw consumption; exact common uniforms for ties are **not** claimed. Customer/public RFQ tape and price-path hashes must agree across policies for every paired session. CRN means matched exogenous histories, not identical PnL paths. Source, input, config, policy and output SHA-256 manifests accompany results.

Pre-pilot scheduling repair: the research-loaded participant module receives a deterministic logical `time.time()` clock and the published `closes_at` deadlines. The clock advances 0.5 seconds per official price update and to each matching deadline. This makes remaining-time features decrease as in the real runner; wall execution speed never substitutes for market time. Only the research-loaded participant module binding is injected; no organiser source or global Python time function is modified. This repair was made before tuning or held-out evaluation.
