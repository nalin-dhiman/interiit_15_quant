# Response estimation completion: prospective protocol

This stage closes an estimation question in a declared synthetic law. It does
not enable SDK R3, claim market identification, or require competition releases.
The earlier failed logistic calibration and all its validation outcomes remain
counterevidence. A new model is evaluated only on new independent seeds.

## Question and information

Can a simple, suitably conditional finite fill estimate calibrate better than
the previous misspecified logistic fit? The target is own winning **and**
eligibility. One final observation is used per RFQ/experimental treatment arm;
callbacks are not samples. A losing quote cannot identify its hidden customer
limit or separate competition from eligibility. The unmodified organiser Engine
generates outcomes, including its binary-float eligibility arithmetic and ties.

Training, development, validation and outside-domain experiments have disjoint
SHA-256 seed namespaces. Exogenous price/customer/opponent/exploration streams
are separate. Prices and hidden payloads are synthetically generated by the
existing controlled-intervention harness. Neither supplied demo CSV is opened.
The old failed experiment is not retuned and relabelled as a held-out result.

The controlled law randomizes a final quote edge at each RFQ initial reference;
there is no intermediate public price update before matching. Features are
initial and current edge, public product/side/quantity, previous public price
increment, the assigned experimental mixture parameter, and a public numerical
comparison of our quote with initial reference +/- our own nominal edge. The
last feature tests binary arithmetic; it never reads an individual hidden limit.
Initial/current references coincide here. Different timing, unknown laws,
products, quantities, increments or treatment ranges cause an abstention.

## Frozen comparison

At theta=0.2 and 0.8, use 200 independent training sessions per level, each 120
RFQs. Development uses 40 sessions at each of theta=0.35/0.65. Final validation
uses 100 sessions at each of theta=0.2/0.35/0.65/0.8. An additional 100 sessions
with shifted opponent quote laws are explicitly outside the supported domain;
all prediction methods must abstain there. This is a domain test, not a claim
that those losses were repaired. The assignment parameter is known to the lab;
it is not assumed observable in an arbitrary customer market.

Compare constant, pooled edge, monotone edge, old linear logistic, logistic with
all public features, and conditional empirical finite cells (nominal edge,
public binary-arithmetic comparison). The finite estimator linearly interpolates
between endpoint treatment cells, requiring at least 40 training RFQs at each
endpoint. The conditional mixture is exactly affine under the chosen law;
estimated cell probabilities remain statistically uncertain.

Select the lowest mean development Brier among methods passing all development
levels; fixed method order breaks ties. If none pass, report the lowest Brier
but export no validated artifact. Freeze this choice before final validation.
No failed candidate is removed from the final tables. Gates require full support,
mean calibration error <=0.03, 10-bin ECE <=0.05, supported edge-bin error <=0.10
and supported public-cell error <=0.15. Supported validation bins contain at
least 100 RFQs. These thresholds are empirical, not simultaneous error bounds.
Export additionally requires the selected method's session-bootstrap 95% Brier
difference interval versus constant to lie below zero. Its intervals condition
on the frozen training fit and assume independent sessions from this generator.

Post-fill price-change regression uses past signed increments and product;
future prices appear only as labels. The deliberate 0.4 momentum in this
synthetic generator can make that regression useful. This cannot establish
transport to different basis/price dynamics or actual customer markets.

## Response comparison and closure

Use 80 fresh primitive sessions, each rerun at theta=0.45/0.50/0.55, with matched
exogenous primitives and the original SDK tie RNG. Every estimated method gets
the same three-arm information and Engine budget. The fee is 0.01+0.01*theta;
the explicit reward derivative -0.01*quantity*own-fill is retained. The lab
records its own assigned primitive family, so its likelihood and conditional
refit comparisons have information not generally present in passive SDK data.
That metadata never enters the fill predictor above.

Report local/full-path likelihood, paired finite contrast, known numerical-law
tangent, estimated finite tangent and direct conditional refit. The last two
use the same cell probabilities and therefore should agree algebraically in
this affine-probability/affine-fee family. Such agreement supplies no extra
response information, compute advantage, or policy alpha. Timings and consumed
Engine executions are recorded; no equal-CPU superiority is asserted. Scores
apply to the common primitive history, never to incompatible deterministic cash
paths. Theta=0/1 is excluded; zero own-fill probability is allowed without a
Bernoulli division by zero. Altered targets, tick changes and deterministic
policy switches are outside this derivative domain.

The earlier reachable finite-model response study remains applicable to its
stated exact family. Any new mathematically certified finite response guard is
reported separately from empirical fit uncertainty. SDK R3 remains disabled:
passing fill calibration alone cannot prove an incremental reset/controller
benefit. The project may finish with a calibrated research artifact and a
negative conclusion about additional response value.

Run `python -m experiments.research_response declare --out <new-dir>` and then
`python -m experiments.research_response execute --out <same-dir>`. The source
and protocol hashes are checked before and after execution, and raw outcomes,
all candidate results, selected-model seal, environments and manifests are saved.
