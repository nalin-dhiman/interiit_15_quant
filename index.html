THE MODEL, IN PLAIN WORDS

Carry risk.
Pay to change it.

01

A fill moves inventory and cash.

When a customer buys, the dealer sells. When a customer sells, the dealer buys. An exchange intervention follows the same accounting and pays its execution costs.

02

A reset is a trade, not an eraser.

The continuation policy compares retaining exposure with feasible inventory targets. A partial reset changes the position; previous losses and the market history remain.

03

Settlement and predictability matter.

Instrument B settles to twice A. The difference between B and twice A is the public basis. A conventional controller can use that information too; a positive result does not by itself identify an advantage from resetting.
